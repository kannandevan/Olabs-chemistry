When the electrodes are made to touch each other, The Bulb glows
When the electrodes are placed with the maximum gap, The Bulb doesn't glow.
When the electrodes are placed with the minimum gap, The Bulb glows.
When the electrodes are placed with the maximum gap and with addition of Sodium Chloride, The Bulb glows.
The higher resistance in the distilled water with the maximum gap between the electrodes, the LED does not glow but when the solution of a salt whose molecules split into anions and cations becomes less resistive. This is because when concentration rises, more charge carriers (+ve and -ve ions) become available and move through the electric field and the LED glows.
