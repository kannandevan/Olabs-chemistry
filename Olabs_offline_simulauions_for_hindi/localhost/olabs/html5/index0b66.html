The Metal Can should not touch the knob or the body of the Electroscope.
If there is no change in deflection in the Charged Electroscope, it means that the earthed Metal Can is providing an electrostatic field to the Charged Electroscope. The presence of a Charged Ebonite or Glass Rod near the knob of the Electroscope or the outside of the Metal Can, can hardly affect the leaves of the Electroscope. This is Electrostatic Shielding.
