Sulphur

Take care that the spoon should not dip in the water.


  • Instructions


    1.Drag and drop the lighter towards the spirit lamp to burn it.


    2.Click the bottle cap to open the sulphur bottle.


    3.Drag and drop the spoon towards the bottle.


    4.Drag and drop the spoon with sulphur powder towards the spirit lamp to burn the powder.


    5.Wait till the sulphur powder is completely dissolved and forms a burning sulphur.


    6.Drag and drop the spoon towards the gas jar and wait for few minutes to see the gas gets collected inside the gas jar.


    7.Click on the spoon.


    8.Click on the gas jar to shake it well to get the sulphur gas get dissolved completely.


    9.Click on the lid to open the gas jar.


    10.Drag and drop the gas jar towards the watch glass and pour the water on to it.


    11.Drag and drop the red litmus paper on to the watch glass.


    12.Drag and drop the blue litmus paper on to the watch glass.
